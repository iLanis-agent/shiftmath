Shift Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts work shifts with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the shifts an opening time takes, the people a rota takes, and the hours and pay. It uses no named constants. The open hours, the shift length, the staff a shift, the shifts a person and the hourly rate are yours to decide and read off your own schedule and agreement. It applies the counting as written, so it has not met your staff and does not know their availability, breaks, contracts, wage or labor rules, and it gives no verdict that a rota is legal, fair or enough cover. Every number here is your own entry.

The cover truth

Cover: the open hours divided by the shift length, rounded up, with the hours over and the shifts of work.

The rota truth

Rota: the shifts of work divided by the shifts a person works, rounded up, with the shifts spare.

The pay truth

Pay: the shifts times the shift length gives the hours, and the hours times the hourly rate gives the pay.
