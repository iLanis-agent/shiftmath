Shift Math

The 14 open hours in shifts of 4 hours take 4 shifts, covering 16 hours with 2 over, and with 3 staff a shift that is 12 shifts of work.

Boundary: arithmetic only Shift Math counts work shifts with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the shifts an opening time takes, the people a rota takes, and the hours and pay. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not met your staff and does not know their availability, breaks, contracts, wage or labor rules, and it gives no verdict that a rota is legal, fair or enough cover. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 14 open hours, shifts of 4 hours, 3 staff a shift, 5 shifts a person and $15.50 an hour.
